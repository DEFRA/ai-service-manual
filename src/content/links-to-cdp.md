## Getting support

If you're getting started with integrating AI into your service, your first point of call is the AI Capabilities & Enablement team.

Reach out for support in the #ask-ace channel.

## CDP resources

CDP is integrated with Amazon Bedrock.

The integration includes:

- various models
- guardrails and safety controls
- cost management and tracking

This integration is growing and changing. Check the [CDP documentation](https://portal.cdp-int.defra.cloud/documentation/how-to/bedrock-ai.md) for the latest information.

For support, reach out in:

- #ask-ace on Slack
- CDP support channel

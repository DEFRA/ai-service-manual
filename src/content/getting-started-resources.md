If you're just getting started with AI, you're in the right place. This page brings together the most useful resources to help you begin safely and confidently. If you ever get stuck or have a question, the team is on the **#ask-ace** channel in Slack and happy to help.

## Start here — using AI safely at Defra

The most important thing when using AI at Defra is doing so safely and securely. Before exploring tools or learning resources, make sure you understand what is expected of you.

The [AI Training & Guidance page on SharePoint](https://defra.sharepoint.com/teams/Team4303/SitePages/AI-Training-&-Guidance.aspx) is the best place to start. It includes Civil Service learning courses covering safe and responsible AI use.

For AI policy and governance across Defra, visit the [AI Governance page on SharePoint](https://defra.sharepoint.com/teams/Team4303/SitePages/AI-Governance.aspx).

Once you have a grounding in safe use, head to our [Getting started with AI](/getting-started-with-ai) guide for practical guidance on using AI in your role.

## Learning resources from the main AI providers

For up-to-date getting started guides from the main AI providers, we recommend:

- **Microsoft** — [Microsoft Copilot learning hub](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/) — the central home for all Copilot training, organised by product.
- **Claude (Anthropic)** — [Getting started with Claude](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8114491-getting-started-with-claude) — how to use Claude's chat interface, with tips for getting better results.
- **AWS** — [AWS AI training](https://aws.amazon.com/training/learn-about/ai/) — free courses covering AI fundamentals, designed for all roles and levels.
- **Google** — [Gemini Prompt Guide for Google Workspace](https://workspace.google.com/learning/content/gemini-prompt-guide) — a role-based prompting guide with concrete examples for everyday tasks.
- **OpenAI** — [OpenAI Academy content hub](https://academy.openai.com/public/content) — videos, articles, and prompt packs organised by role and use case.

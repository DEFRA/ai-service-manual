## Role-specific onboarding guides

We are creating onboarding guides for each DDAT profession in Defra that cover how to use AI effectively in your specific role. These guides are coming soon. Get in touch if you would like to know more.

### Defra guidance

The AI playbook sets out how Defra approaches AI in the software delivery lifecycle. The approved tools list shows which AI tools are currently available to pilot teams.

- [AI playbook](https://defra.github.io/defra-ai-sdlc)
- [Approved tools for AI pilot teams](https://defra.github.io/ai-sdlc-tool-guidance/)

### Government guidance

The UK Government AI Playbook provides a broader view of how government departments should approach AI adoption and governance.

- [UK Government AI Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-playbook-for-the-uk-government)

### Prompt engineering

Prompt engineering is one of the most practical skills you can develop. These resources cover the basics and go into detail on how to get better results from AI.

- [Prompt Engineering Masterclass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBD2FtxNOEM) (video)
- [Google: Intro to prompt engineering](https://cloud.google.com/discover/what-is-prompt-engineering?hl=en)
- [Amazon: What is prompt engineering](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/prompt-engineering/)

### Generative AI

For a broader introduction to generative AI and how it works, this course from Microsoft covers the fundamentals.

- [Microsoft: Generative AI for beginners](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/shows/generative-ai-for-beginners/)
